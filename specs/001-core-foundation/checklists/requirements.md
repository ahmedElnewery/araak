Permanent mailbox-loss procedure APPROVED (CL-013): disable old account and revoke access; invite a new account with a new email and internal identity; retain old records/audits; no automatic record ownership handover. Replacement roles chosen explicitly afresh without copying/prefilling from the old account APPROVED (CL-014). Manager-assisted reset APPROVED (CL-015): authorized manager confirms with the **manager's own current password** via authoritative auth; employee alone sets the new password through a secure tenant-bound recovery flow; no manager OTP, no password visibility, and all old employee sessions are revoked on completion. One-hour (60-minute) single-use validity for both self-service and manager-initiated password reset APPROVED (CL-016). Immediately invalidate ALL older password-reset links upon issuance of any new link for the same staff account across self-service/manager paths APPROVED (CL-017); the newest link alone receives a fresh 60-minute single-use window. Five-minute (300-second) per-account issuance cooldown across self-service and manager-assisted requests APPROVED (CL-018); cooldown-denied requests do not issue new links or invalidate existing links. At most FIVE successfully issued links per account within any rolling 24 hours across both paths APPROVED (CL-019); rejected attempts neither count nor invalidate earlier links. Secure provider-compatible expiry/reissue, cooldown and rolling-cap enforcement must be proven; delivery/authorization details plus IP/global anti-abuse safeguards remain OPEN. Standalone manual invitation cancellation is OUT of MVP scope (CL-020; latest B overrides earlier A); expiry, redemption or authorized resend are the only invitation invalidation routes. Invitation Resend cooldown of 15 minutes (900 seconds) since last successful issuance/reissue APPROVED (CL-021); premature requests leave the existing invitation valid and unchanged, with concurrency-safe server enforcement. Maximum FIVE successful resends per pending invitation in any rolling 24-hour window APPROVED (CL-022), excluding first invitation issuance; rejected requests have no side effects, and concurrency-safe tenant-local counters are required. Initial email verification, broader anti-abuse controls, and other recovery lifecycle details remain unresolved. Verify secure seven-day expiry and resend enforcement in the Authentication Plan.
- [ ] **G-03:** Protected tenant-manager appointment APPROVED (CL-023): authorized platform Super Admin only, including invitation and promotion; tenant managers/delegates cannot mint manager authority via roles or APIs, and platform role does not grant private tenant-data access. Manager-role revocation APPROVED (CL-024): only authorized platform Super Admin may revoke/remove tenant-manager authority, with no tenant-admin or API bypass; prevent revocation of an active tenant's last activated, active manager, and audit every action. Manager-demotion account policy APPROVED (CL-025): account stays enabled, non-manager roles and staff identity are retained, manager-only grants removed immediately even for existing sessions; no default role is added. Manager-demotion session policy APPROVED (CL-026): immediately revoke ALL existing sessions and refresh capabilities across devices, require fresh login before staff continues under the remaining roles. Role Management self-edit prohibition APPROVED (CL-027): any tenant role administrator (including a manager/delegate) cannot edit a role they belong to, even for non-escalating edits; enforce server-side across APIs with concurrency-safe membership checks and audit. Deleting a role still assigned to any employee is PROHIBITED (CL-028), including disabled employee assignments; all memberships must first be explicitly removed via authorized audited operations, with server-side atomic checks and no automatic cascade. Reversible role Archive instead of permanent deletion APPROVED (CL-029), only after all staff assignments have been explicitly removed. Archived roles preserve their identity and history, are excluded from active role pickers, cannot be assigned, and may be restored without automatic reassignment. Restore Role authority APPROVED (CL-030): a tenant manager within grant authority or a delegated staff administrator explicitly granted `Restore Role` with sufficient authority for the archived role's complete permissions/scopes. Generic Role Management alone does not grant restoration rights; prevent cross-tenant/protected-manager grants and audit unauthorized requests. Restoring a role never automatically reassigns former employees. Role-name reuse APPROVED (CL-031): an archived role's name can be used for a new role, but active role names must remain unique within a tenant. Restoration is blocked on an active-name collision until an authorized rename of one role resolves it; never automatically rename, merge or reassign members. Enforce atomically in server-side create/rename/restore flows and audit decisions. Audit-viewer scope APPROVED (CL-032): delegated `audit_logs.view` users see only explicitly authorized event categories (such as staff, roles or settings), never all tenant events by default. Enforce tenant/category filters in server-side list, detail, search, counts, pagination and exports, prevent platform/other-tenant/private-record leakage, and revalidate when privileges change. Administrative Audit Log retention APPROVED (CL-033): retain staff, roles, invitation and tenant administrative events for 12 months after creation, with tenant-safe audited expiry and legal-preservation exceptions. Security Logs retention APPROVED separately (CL-034): keep failed authentication, unauthorized access and privilege-bypass security events for three months from event time, without sensitive secrets, using tenant-scoped safe expiry and applicable lawful preservation exceptions. Domain records, backup retention and remaining classes are still unresolved. Security Logs visibility APPROVED (CL-035): only an authorized tenant manager can view their own tenant security events. Staff delegation is prohibited even when staff have `audit_logs.view`; verify tenant and manager checks for all UI/API read/search/count/export operations, redact secrets and block cross-tenant/platform events. Session meaningful activity APPROVED (CL-036): only user-initiated, successfully authenticated and authorized server requests such as protected navigation, explicit protected-data reads or saves reset the 30-day inactivity timer. Background token refresh, polling, mouse movement, unsubmitted typing, denied/expired requests and passive requests do not reset it. Enforce server-side. Session detail display APPROVED (CL-037; modified B): show device type, browser, login timestamp, last verified meaningful activity and Current Session label, with controls to revoke other sessions. No IP address, GPS, IP-derived approximate location or other geographic location is displayed or requested in this feature. Preserve own-account/tenant scoping, unknown-device fallback and no token exposure. Individual other-session Logout/Revoke confirmation APPROVED (CL-038): show a clear confirmation prompt; Confirm immediately revokes only the chosen other session and its refresh capability, without current-password re-entry or OTP. Cancel leaves all sessions unchanged. Enforce session ownership, tenant checks, current-session preservation, concurrent refresh safety and audit. `Logout All Other Sessions` confirmation APPROVED (CL-039): prompt with explicit warning that every other device/session will be logged out while the current session stays active; Confirm immediately invalidates all other sessions and refresh capabilities without password re-entry or OTP, Cancel changes nothing. Enforce authorized account/tenant scope, refresh-race safety and audit; keep this user-initiated action distinct from mandatory all-device revocations. Additional platform Super Admin invitation APPROVED (CL-040): only an active platform Super Admin with explicit `platform_admins.invite` authority may invite a new platform Super Admin through the platform administration UI. The invite is secure and non-public, activated only after recipient acceptance, server-authorized and recorded in platform audit history; tenant roles cannot grant platform authority, and new platform administrators get no default tenant-private data access. Platform Super Admin revocation APPROVED (CL-041; choice B): platform admins cannot remove/demote each other via admin UI, public or application APIs, even if they can invite Super Admins. Removal requires an authorized, restricted and audited platform-operations technical procedure; withdrawn platform privileges stop authorizing protected requests immediately. Preserve normal administrative continuity and document emergency recovery/session invalidation separately. Platform Super Admin invitation expiry APPROVED (CL-042; choice A): newly issued, recipient-bound single-use invitations are valid strictly before the 24-hour (86,400-second) boundary from issuance. Any acceptance at/after expiry or repeated redemption MUST fail without activating privilege; enforce on backend independently of provider token validity and audit outcomes. Seven-day tenant staff invitation expiry remains unchanged. First platform-admin bootstrap APPROVED (CL-043): create the first Super Admin once using a documented secure platform-operations procedure, not a public/admin setup UI; require authorized operators, secure credentials, audit, and prevention of repeated/concurrent extra administrator creation. Later admins use the existing-admin invitation flow. Staff self-role-assignment restriction APPROVED (CL-044; choice A): a tenant employee, including a tenant manager or role-management delegate, may NEVER add, remove, swap or edit their OWN staff-role memberships, even when the change would only reduce permissions. A separate appropriately authorized actor may modify ordinary roles within grant authority; protected tenant-manager grant/revocation remains platform-only. Enforce actor/target ID checks on the server in single/bulk/UI/API flows and audit attempted bypasses. This complements the separate prohibition on editing an assigned role's definition (CL-027). Backup Restore execution APPROVED (CL-045; choice A): tenant managers may request recovery, but only authorized platform operations staff may execute a restore through a controlled, audited operations procedure; no tenant or Super Admin UI/API self-service restore. Verify authorization, backup integrity, tenant scope, shared-database cross-tenant impact, approvals and recovery evidence before any Production overwrite. Backup retention APPROVED (CL-046; choice A): retain DAILY recoverable database backups and required stored-file backups across a rolling 30-day window. Verify encrypted/restricted storage, separate file-object recovery, provider-plan feasibility, protected expiry and isolated restore before Production; do not assume database backups include files. Restore rehearsal frequency APPROVED (CL-047; choice A): perform and document an isolated database-plus-required-files restore test before the first Production launch and at least every three calendar months afterward, recording operator, proof of integrity/tenant isolation and failure remediation. A missing or failed initial test blocks go-live; later failures require corrective action and a repeat test. Detailed RPO/RTO objectives remain separate operations-plan and launch-gate decisions. Super Admin invitation resend/reissue and remaining grant-authority/operational implementation details remain OPEN.
